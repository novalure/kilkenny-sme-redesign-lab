# V1 visual audit — 1 October 2026

Reviewed rendered V1 at 375, 390, 768, 1024 and 1440 px before V2 work. Screenshots captured locally; desktop and mobile first views also retained at `/workspace/yvonne-ross-desktop-preview.png` and `/workspace/yvonne-ross-mobile-preview.png`.

| Finding | Consequence | V2 action |
|---|---|---|
| Warm paper, moss and bronze cover most of every viewport | Reads as heritage retail | Use graphite, porcelain and silver; reserve sapphire for a few controls |
| Cormorant serif dominates every headline, wordmark and story | Classical editorial mood persists on every route | Use a modern variable grotesk for display and body, with tighter architectural spacing |
| Hero divides text and illustrated ring into conventional equal panels | First screen resembles a traditional shop template | Full-bleed dark art direction with integrated oversized type and short copy |
| At 375/390 the rating sits below a tall hero and is missed in the first view | Strongest social proof arrives too late | Put live-or-dated Google rating in the hero, then give it a dedicated editorial section |
| Three equal product cards and small centered ring drawings | Feels like generic catalogue | Build a wide swipeable editorial reel with varying crop/scale and clear official product links |
| Review block is a small late-page metric | Trust signal lacks presence | Large typographic rating, Google attribution and click-through near the top |
| Designer and studio use similar two-column split modules | Page rhythm is predictable, especially at 768/1024 | Alternate full-bleed, asymmetric, and typographic sections |
| Repeated green rectangular CTAs and tiny uppercase labels | Visual grammar is conservative | Use a deliberate mix of solid, line and text actions with a single blue detail |

No V1 overflow was observed at the five audited widths; contact, map, review link and sticky call are sound foundations to keep.
