# V2 visual benchmarks — 1 October 2026

Current rendered desktop views were inspected for [Tiffany & Co.](https://www.tiffany.com/), [Shaun Leane](https://shaunleane.com/), [Anna Sheffield](https://www.annasheffield.com/) and the [Awwwards jewellery directory](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/jewelry/). Cartier blocked automated access, so no visual claim is made about its current site. These are visual references, not sources of Yvonne Ross facts or reusable media.

| Pattern observed | Why it works | Application to Yvonne | Do not copy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shaun Leane gives nearly the entire opening viewport to one tightly cropped campaign image | Immediate design and material presence | One sculptural, full-bleed concept composition carrying a small amount of type | Its model photography, over-image navigation, warm grading or campaign identity |
| Anna Sheffield opens with two bold, adjacent macro product crops | Product geometry reads at a distance | Large alternating visual panels and a gallery with different scales | Product images, split product layout or teal palette |
| Tiffany gives a campaign visual ample space while preserving a direct collection action | Clear route from editorial story to exploration | Strong primary jewellery action and a second studio/contact action | Brand typography, color, photography or navigation scale |
| Awwwards jewellery examples use highly varied photo crops and modular composition | Distinct narrative rhythm avoids catalogue monotony | Alternate dark macro, light type-led, reel, metric and location modules | Any particular award entry's composition or interaction |
| Across inspected sites, visible navigation and concise actions stay close to campaign media | Immersion retains utility | Contact remains explicit on desktop; call remains persistent on mobile | Generic icon toolbar or complex megamenu |

The V2 uses original CSS concept art because no reuse rights for Yvonne's product or portrait photography were supplied. It cannot yet match the tactile quality of authorised macro photography; media slots are designed to accept future assets without changing section structure.
