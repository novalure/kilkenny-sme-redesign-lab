# Yvonne Ross concept rationale

**Goal and audience.** A digital atelier for people seeking jewellery, private commissions, engagement and wedding rings, and a physical Kilkenny studio visit. The concept supports direct conversation as well as shop discovery.

**Hierarchy.** Desktop promotes Contact Yvonne in the header; mobile fixes Call the Studio at the bottom and keeps directions and enquiry accessible in the page. The homepage leads from editorial introduction through discovery, selected pieces, designer story, commissions, rings, craft, Google trust, studio and contact.

**Visual system.** Warm paper, moss green, charcoal and soft metal tones. Cormorant Garamond provides expressive but restrained display type; Manrope provides clear navigation and body copy. Abstract original artwork occupies explicit media slots until authorised photography is available. The prominent typographic name is a concept treatment, not a replacement for an authorised logo.

**Contact.** A dedicated route presents studio details, phone, email, directions and a short form. The form validates fields in the browser, requires a phone number if that channel is chosen, then shows a demo notice. It sends no request and stores nothing.

**Reviews and map.** A dated 4.6 Google snapshot and link offer restrained trust. Count and testimonials are omitted because they were not currently verified. Leaflet shows OpenStreetMap tiles with visible attribution, accessible adjacent directions, and a fallback.

**Higgsfield and rights.** The media plan defines slots for later creative production. No official photography has been reused. Illustrations are explicitly labelled as concept visuals and are never represented as inventory. Prior to public release, verify image licences, replace key placeholders, refresh rating and resolve the Eircode conflict.

**Public concept safety.** All pages identify the work as an unofficial redesign concept, use noindex/nofollow plus robots disallow, make no payment flow and send no enquiry to Yvonne Ross. Public deployment remains a separate decision.
