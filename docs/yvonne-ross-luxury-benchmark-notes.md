# Luxury jewellery journey notes

Checked 1 October 2026. These are navigation and journey principles, not visual references or copy sources.

| Site | Observed journey principle | Concept application |
|---|---|---|
| Tiffany & Co. — https://www.tiffany.com/ | Engagement and wedding are distinct routes; appointment prompts appear in the shopping journey. | Give rings a dedicated route and a direct consultation CTA. |
| Boucheron — https://www.boucheron.com/us/ | Book a visit, prepare an in-store visit, and find a boutique are explicit actions. | Make the physical studio a major home section with directions and contact. |
| FoundRae — https://foundrae.com/ | Storytelling and store/appointment paths coexist with collection discovery. | Pair the designer story with clear next steps. |
| Shaun Leane — https://www.shaunleane.com/ | Engagement, wedding, bespoke experiences and contact are separately discoverable. | Keep bespoke and rings legible outside the general shop. |
| Cartier — https://www.cartier.com/en-us/ | Automated access returned 403 on the verification date. | No current-page observation used. |
| David Yurman — https://www.davidyurman.com/ | Automated access returned a client challenge. | No current-page observation used. |

The concept uses these journey patterns while keeping Yvonne Ross's own studio, product facts and visual identity central. No benchmark images or text were copied.
