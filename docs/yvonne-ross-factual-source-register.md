## Conflicts and omissions

- **Eircode:** The official site footer shows **R95 FH9X**; Google Maps shows **R95 VXD0**; OpenStreetMap Nominatim shows **R95 F954** for the building. The concept omits the Eircode and uses the agreed street and city. Verify with the business before launch.
- **Floor:** Google Maps says "1st Floor" while the official site says 19 Rose Inn Street without a floor. The concept omits the floor.
- **Opening hours:** Google Maps shows a dynamic open/closing state, while the official site only states Tuesday–Saturday. The concept does not publish unverified hours.
- **Review count:** An earlier supplied note cited eight, but a current count could not be independently verified. The concept omits the count.
- **Awards:** The official home page calls Yvonne award winning, but specific awards were not independently checked, so the concept omits the claim.
- **Biography and tenure:** No years of experience, qualifications other than the official Gem-A claim, or biographical details are added.
