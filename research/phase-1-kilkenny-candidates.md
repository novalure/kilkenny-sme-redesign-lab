## Screening notes

The 24 rejected during screening were businesses with no verifiable current website, apparently closed or stale listings, duplicated/centrally controlled chain brands, or sites that appeared already strong enough for this preliminary redesign-candidate pool. Rejection was conservative: it does not mean the business is poor, only that it was not accepted for this phase’s shortlist.

## TOP CANDIDATES FOR HUMAN REVIEW

### Cakeface

- Business: Cakeface
- Category: Bakery/café
- Location: Kilkenny City
- Website: https://cakeface.ie/

The business has a strong local, visual product proposition and an identifiable website. It may merit closer review because ordering/enquiry information and practical visit details could be brought closer to the first screen and made easier to scan on mobile.

### Kilkenny Café

- Business: Kilkenny Café
- Category: Café
- Location: Kilkenny City
- Website: https://kilkennycafe.ie/

This is a small, owner-style hospitality candidate with a focused local offer. A review could test whether menu, opening hours, booking/contact and atmosphere are communicated quickly enough for a visitor deciding where to go.

### Made in Kilkenny

- Business: Made in Kilkenny
- Category: Local gifts/craft retail
- Location: Kilkenny City
- Website: https://madeinkilkenny.ie/

The local-maker proposition is well suited to stronger storytelling and visual merchandising. The site may benefit from clearer maker/product discovery, while retaining a straightforward route to shop or visit.

### The Flower Shop Kilkenny

- Business: The Flower Shop Kilkenny
- Category: Florist
- Location: Kilkenny City
- Website: https://www.theflowershopkilkenny.com/

Florists depend on quick occasion, delivery and ordering decisions, making this a useful SME candidate. A closer review could examine whether those paths, delivery coverage and trust information are sufficiently prominent.

### Butler House

- Business: Butler House
- Category: Guesthouse/wedding venue
- Location: Kilkenny City
- Website: https://butlerhouse.net/

The heritage setting offers strong visual and experiential material. Because accommodation and events serve different audiences, the site may merit review for clearer journeys, booking confidence and mobile prioritisation.

### The Tower House B&B

- Business: The Tower House B&B
- Category: B&B
- Location: Thomastown
- Website: https://www.towerhousebandb.com/

This is a smaller county-Kilkenny accommodation business with a direct booking opportunity. A review could focus on room information, availability/contact, directions and how quickly the site establishes trust.

### Kilkenny Walking Tours

- Business: Kilkenny Walking Tours
- Category: Tourism/tours
- Location: Kilkenny City
- Website: https://kilkennywalkingtours.ie/

The offer is clear and locally distinctive, but tour businesses need especially frictionless schedule and booking information. It may be worth reviewing how guide credibility, meeting points and practical planning are presented.

### Kilkenny Gym

- Business: Kilkenny Gym
- Category: Independent gym
- Location: Kilkenny City
- Website: https://www.kilkennygym.com/

An independent gym can convert well through a clear trial or membership journey. The candidate merits review for the visibility of pricing, timetable, facilities, testimonials and first-visit CTA.

### Kilkenny Stone & Marble

- Business: Kilkenny Stone & Marble
- Category: Home/building materials
- Location: County Kilkenny
- Website: https://www.kilkennystone.ie/

This is a product-and-project business where better imagery and clearer quote pathways could materially help prospective customers. A human review could assess whether materials, applications, service area and proof of work are easy to understand.

### T.J. Reid Builders

- Business: T.J. Reid Builders
- Category: Builder/trade
- Location: County Kilkenny
- Website: https://www.tjreidbuilders.com/

The business appears suitable for a trust-led local-services redesign conversation, subject to manual confirmation. The most useful review points are project evidence, credentials, service area, process and the route to request a quote.
