# Phase 3 — Strict bad/outdated website discovery

Verified: 2026-10-01 (Europe/Vienna)

## Result

- Businesses visually screened: **40**
- Accepted: **15**
- **A — Prime redesign targets: 12**
- **B — Possible redesign targets: 3**
- Rejected because the website was already too good for this strategy: **14**
- Other rejections (dead/inconsistent site, duplicate identity, insufficient evidence, or weak outreach fit): **11**

The selection logic was reset for this phase. A site was not accepted for minor navigation, hierarchy or conversion opportunities. Every accepted site scored **5/10 or lower for current visual quality**, **8/10 or higher for before/after potential**, **7/10 or higher for SME outreach fit**, and **6/10 or higher for content availability**.

Serious candidates were opened directly and visually inspected at approximately 1440×1000 desktop and 390×844 mobile viewports. The review considered the rendered header/hero, navigation, typography, spacing, imagery, CTA treatment, content structure and footer—not just page text or metadata. No screenshots or third-party assets were copied into the repository.

## Other screened rejections

Eleven further businesses were excluded because the direct page was dead or inconsistent, the rendered page remained a loading/error state, the business/site identity was duplicated or uncertain, or the owner-level outreach fit/content evidence did not meet the mandatory threshold. Examples include Campagne/Ember availability failures, the duplicate Kilkenny roofing identity, Boston Barber Bars' multi-location/poor owner-fit case, and pages that did not render enough verifiable business content during inspection. They were not promoted merely to reach the target count.

## Final strict check

For every accepted business, the rendered current site shows several visible legacy or presentation weaknesses—not one minor defect—and a modern concept can be grounded in public factual content. The first manual review should start with **Urlingford Arms Hotel, Forget Me Not Florist, Yvonne Ross Jewellery, Kevin Moore Building Contractor, The Hub Thomastown, Philip Holohan Kitchens and Frisby Kitchens & Wardrobes**, where the visual gap is clearest.
