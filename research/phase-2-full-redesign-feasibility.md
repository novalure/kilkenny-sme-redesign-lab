# Phase 2 — Full redesign feasibility review

Checked: 2026-10-01 (Europe/Vienna)

## Outcome

- Businesses reviewed: **32 of 32**
- **A — Excellent redesign candidate: 7**
- **B — Good redesign candidate: 4**
- **C — Possible, but with limitations: 4**
- **D — Do not redesign: 17**

This is a focused redesign-feasibility review, not a technical, SEO or performance audit. The official Phase 1 URL was opened for every candidate. For live sites, the homepage and relevant menu, product/service, about, contact, booking, room, tour or portfolio pages were inspected where available. Where the Phase 1 domain failed, public search was used only to locate a possible replacement official domain and verify identity; no substitute business was silently adopted.

The review exposed a material Phase 1 data-quality issue: several accepted entries have dead domains, duplicate another candidate, or resolve in search to a differently named or differently located business. Those entries are retained because Phase 2 must cover all 32, but are classified D where the named SME cannot be verified. Phase 1's correct summary remains **54 discovered, 32 accepted, 22 rejected**.

Score order in the table is: **Current website quality / Transformation potential / Content availability / Visual feasibility / Commercial relevance / Demo build feasibility / SME outreach fit**. A high current-quality score is positive for the existing site but usually reduces transformation potential.

## BEST REDESIGN OPPORTUNITIES FOR FRANZ + CHATGPT

These are all **A — Excellent redesign candidate** entries. This is not a final selection.

### Campagne

The broken official experience creates a dramatic before/after opportunity for a highly commercial restaurant journey. Verified public menu and Michelin information provide a usable factual base, but original or licensed concept imagery and cautious unofficial-demo labelling are essential.

### The Grapevine

This is one of the clearest small-business opportunities: abundant real menu, booking, voucher, gallery and story content sits inside an overlong, repetitive structure. A mobile-first concept could visibly improve both atmosphere and reservation clarity without inventing the business.

### La Rivista

Restaurant, market, takeaway and catering are all real, current offers with extensive menus. The transformation opportunity is to turn fragmented navigation into a coherent food brand and make each commercial journey immediately understandable.

### Made in Kilkenny

The maker collective has excellent authentic content, strong visual subject matter and a genuine county-wide discovery story. A redesigned maker/trail/experience journey could be distinctive and credible, subject to collective governance and image permissions.

### Kilkenny Walking Tours

The owner-led tour business provides prices, tour descriptions, a guide story, meeting information, FAQs and awards—enough to design with confidence. Dense presentation and stale schedule wording make the potential improvement unusually clear; live dates would need confirmation before publication.

### Kilkenny Chiropractic Clinic

Detailed team biographies, qualifications and clinic history create a strong trust base. The opportunity is primarily information architecture: a reassuring first-visit, team, services and contact experience using careful health language and no invented outcomes.

### Kilkenny Roofing

The live replacement site has clear services and a direct commercial purpose but weak differentiation, proof and visual credibility. It can support a convincing contractor concept, although Franz should verify which of the similar domains is the actual outreach target before any demo or contact.

## SECONDARY OPTIONS

These are all **B — Good redesign candidate** entries.

- **Cakeface** — Excellent brand, founder story and product/event material, but the current site is already capable and includes commerce. Worth considering only if the concept solves its competing visit, shop, school and event paths.
- **Rive Gauche** — Strong restaurant visuals and complete factual content support a good concept. The gap is meaningful but less dramatic than The Grapevine or La Rivista.
- **Butler House** — Exceptional rooms, heritage, dining, garden and event content with a genuine navigation problem. Booking complexity and Kilkenny Civic Trust ownership make outreach less straightforward than a typical SME.
- **Kilkenny Cycling Tours** — Rich tour/rental information and strong commercial relevance, but the current booking-oriented site is already competent. A concept would need superior route storytelling and experience comparison.

## Recommendation for manual review

Start with the seven A candidates, but perform a short identity/rights confirmation before authorising any public concept. The strongest low-complexity, owner-style prospects in this evidence set are **The Grapevine, La Rivista, Kilkenny Walking Tours and Kilkenny Chiropractic Clinic**. Campagne has exceptional transformation potential but more image and availability risk; Made in Kilkenny has governance considerations; Kilkenny Roofing needs identity verification across its similar domains.
